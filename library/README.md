## Before using a photo in a video
- Skip frames with readable car plates, names on receipts or cups, and recognisable people.
- Photos that look AI-generated should not be used if the video claims "real photos".
- Brand ads on screens/stores (Cartier, Dior, Louis Vuitton) read as endorsements; crop them out or avoid.
